# Open Estia — a community's open invitation

**Brand:** Open Estia

**Tagline:** Find your people and build

The story begins with the global Greek community: people who want to find each other, offer practical support and grow together. It welcomes the wider builder community without requiring Greek heritage. Philoxenia by Hustle Hours is the inspiration; Open Estia is not presented as its official expansion or partner.

## The narrative

The homepage moves from belonging, to Philoxenia's spark, to seeing bigger possibilities by living alongside people with experience. It then describes the worldwide ambition, makes the possible stay concrete, explains the process and limits, and closes with a personal note about starting small. Source-linked news supports this story near the bottom.

The city illustration represents ambition. It does not claim active locations. Chris & Theo and the other host examples remain explicitly fictional. There are no invented testimonials, participant counts, confirmed partnerships or promises of employment or funding.

## The visual language

A large sans-serif opening — **A bigger world. A familiar face.** — sets the tone. English headlines, clear Greek paragraphs, warm paper, pine, restrained rust and amber, thin rules and a small candid photo make the invitation feel personal. A postcard-style thread connects possible cities. An open letter ends the main story.

Typography uses local system sans-serif and Georgia for occasional italic emphasis. There are no remote fonts, animation dependencies, generated portraits or property photographs. The existing doorway/hearth vector mark remains the brand's symbol.

Primary colors: pine #163E35, paper #FAF7F0, ink #17392F, rust #A83C22, amber #EDB15A, muted #586259. `movement.css` handles public design, with shared forms and operator tools in `editorial.css`. Layouts adapt at 1050, 760 and 480 pixels.

The original brand kit remains in `static/brand/`: genuine SVG paths, transparent PNGs, icon and light/dark/monochrome variants. Brand-name availability remains unverified.

## Copy and photographs

Speak plainly about the life someone might share: coffee, work, a couch, a question over the kitchen table. Offer hospitality freely. Explain travel responsibilities and conditional extras without implying a guaranteed placement, job, investment, company access or introduction.

Philoxenia's own story was checked at https://philoxenia.hustlehours.com/el/ on October 21, 2026 (Pacific). It describes Dinos and Takis wanting to move to San Francisco and the difficulty of gaining access. The metaphor is that Philoxenia lit the spark, and Open Estia wants to carry that flame worldwide.
